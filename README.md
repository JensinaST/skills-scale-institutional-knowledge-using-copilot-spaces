## Summary: OctoAcme Project Planning, Roles, and Risk Management

### Project Planning & Team Structure

OctoAcme's **Project Planning** document establishes a structured approach to converting approved initiatives into actionable delivery plans. The process begins with a kickoff meeting and progresses through creating a prioritized backlog with clear acceptance criteria, estimating scope using T-shirt sizing or story points, and defining a Definition of Done. The planning phase also identifies dependencies and integration points across teams, culminating in a release plan and milestone map. Sprint or iteration planning is timeboxed to respect agreed cycles, pulling only items that meet the Definition of Done and have clear acceptance criteria, while respecting team capacity constraints.

### Defining Roles and Responsibilities

The **Roles and Personas** document outlines four key roles that drive project success. **Developers** focus on implementing features to meet acceptance criteria, writing tests, conducting code reviews, and identifying technical risks—with goals centered on delivering reliable code and reducing cycle time. **Product Managers** define what should be built, own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes through data-driven decisions. **Project Managers** coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications to ensure efficient execution. Each role has distinct responsibilities and communication patterns, enabling clear ownership and reducing confusion across cross-functional teams.

### Risk Management & Stakeholder Communication

The **Risk Management & Communication** document provides a systematic approach to identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks throughout the project lifecycle. Teams maintain a Risk Register capturing ID, Description, Impact, Likelihood, Owner, Mitigation Plan, and Status—reviewed regularly at weekly syncs. Beyond risk tracking, the document emphasizes the importance of stakeholder communication through weekly status updates, release announcements, and escalation paths that flow from team-level through PM, Product Lead, and Sponsor. Communication templates for weekly status and incident management ensure transparency, while escalation procedures—including dedicated security incident protocols—keep all parties informed and enable rapid response to blockers and business-impacting issues.
